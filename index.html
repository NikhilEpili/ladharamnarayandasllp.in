About Us

A legacy carried forward, one order at a time

Ladharam Narayandas LLP carries forward a legacy that began in 1957 with Ladharam Narayandas Kirana & General Stores. Built on decades of trust, quality and commitment, we have evolved into a dedicated HoReCa supplier, serving the hospitality industry for over 40 years.

We specialise in supplying a wide range of grocery essentials, imported products, syrups and juices to hotels, restaurants, caterers and other food service businesses. With a portfolio of 900+ products from over 100 trusted brands, we cater to the diverse needs of the hospitality industry.

With a commitment to quality and reliable supply, we aim to be a trusted partner for hospitality businesses across Mumbai.
